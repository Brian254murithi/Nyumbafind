Questions

Frequently asked questions

How are property ratings calculated?

Each property shows the average of all approved tenant reviews. Tenants rate the overall stay plus six categories: condition, location, water reliability, security, value for money and the landlord. Listings with no reviews yet show "Not yet reviewed".

Can I trust the reviews?

Reviews come from registered accounts, one review per tenant per property. Community reports move a review into moderation, and our team verifies or removes it. Look for the verified badge on reviews from confirmed tenants.

How do I list a property?

Register a landlord account, open your dashboard and click "Add property". You can upload a main photo plus up to 11 more, set the rent in KSh, and describe amenities. You can edit or delete your listings at any time.

What should I check before renting?

Run the taps to test water pressure, ask about power backup, check locks and lighting, confirm what the rent covers, and review the lease terms before paying. Ask the NyumbaFind AI assistant for the full checklist.

Are the sample listings real properties?

No. Listings marked "Sample listing" are demonstration data created for development and testing. Genuine listings are created by registered landlords and carry no sample badge. Photographs on sample listings are licensed stock images used as placeholders.

Does NyumbaFind charge rent or take deposits?

No. NyumbaFind is a discovery and reviews platform. All payments, deposits and lease agreements happen directly between you and the landlord — always get a written agreement and a receipt.
